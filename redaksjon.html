Writers

The editorial team

The writers

Alexandra

Alexandra writes Perspektiv: the longer opinion pieces on Milanisti.

She takes a clear stand, critical or positive, and builds it on facts and numbers. She doesn't write to please everyone.

Eric

Eric writes Perspektiv with his eyes on Milan's long history.

He drifts easily back to the old days and the old Milan greatness, and it shows in his writing. When things go well, he recognises the Milan identity. When it goes badly, he says the team and the club are on wild paths. He is sceptical of modern club management and unconventional sporting choices, but he is professional and skilled with words.

Gianni

Gianni writes the match reports and the player ratings.

He follows the game minute by minute and covers the decisive moments and what the coaches said afterwards. He is fair, but not afraid to call things by their name.

Thomas

Thomas writes the Preview.

He waits for the press conference, then gives you team news, the expected line-up and what to watch for. He never gives away anything from the match itself.

Anna

Anna writes News: what is happening around Milan right now.

Contracts, transfers and club matters, with sources openly listed.
