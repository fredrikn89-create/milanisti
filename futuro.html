Club

Milan Futuro

Milan Futuro was founded on 27 June 2024 as AC Milan's reserve side and played its debut season, 2024-25, in Serie C, but was relegated to Serie D after a 2–1 aggregate defeat to SPAL in the relegation play-out. In 2025-26 the team finished 4th in Serie D, Group B, and in 2026-27 it is again playing in Group B.

    Background

    Valeri Vladimirov was born in Plovdiv in Bulgaria on 4 June 2008. He grew up in football at the city's big club Botev Plovdiv, where he played in the academy until 2024.

    He played for Bulgaria's U16 and U17 teams in 2023–2024, and already as a teenager attracted interest from abroad. In 2024 he moved to Milan's academy.

    Career

    At Milan he started in the youth system in 2024 and belongs to the reserve team Milan Futuro. The 2024/25 season (Serie C) brought no appearances, while in 2025/26 (Serie D) he played 19 league matches and scored one goal, in addition to two cup matches.

    He was included in the first-team squad on 17 August 2025 for the Coppa Italia match against Bari, but stayed on the bench. He has no Serie A appearances for the first team as of 10 May 2026. His contract was renewed on 12 June 2026, with no length stated.

    For Bulgaria he has played three matches for the U16s, five for the U17s and six for the U19s (from 2025). No goals for the national teams.
