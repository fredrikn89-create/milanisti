Primavera

Milan's Primavera have won the Campionato Primavera once (1964-65), the Coppa Italia Primavera twice (1984-85, 2009-10) and the Torneo di Viareggio nine times, most recently in 2014. The 2026-27 season opened with a 3–2 home win over Torino.
