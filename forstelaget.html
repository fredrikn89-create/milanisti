    Background

    Pietro Terracciano was born in San Felice a Cancello in Campania. His parents came from Arienzo in the province of Caserta, and that is where he grew up. He believes his great-grandparents were from Avellino, the city of his first football club.

    He came up through Avellino's youth team and was called up to the first team several times in the 2008/09 season. His path then continued through the lower divisions, with spells at Nocerina and Milazzo, before a breakthrough at Catania.

    Career

    Terracciano played for Nocerina (2009–2011) and on loan at Milazzo in 2010/11, before joining Catania in 2011. There he made his Serie A debut on 25 April 2012 away to Cagliari. He was loaned out to Avellino in 2013/14 and later to Salernitana (2015–2017), where he got a lot of playing time.

    In 2017 he went to Empoli, where he was first-choice goalkeeper at the start of the 2018/19 Serie A season before being replaced by Ivan Provedel. In January 2019 he was loaned to Fiorentina, and the club bought him the same summer. He stayed until 2025, and became the club's first-choice goalkeeper from February 2023.

    With Fiorentina he reached the Coppa Italia final in 2022/23 and lost the Conference League finals in both 2022/23 and 2023/24. He made his European debut for Fiorentina in August 2022.

    Milan signed him on 18 July 2025 on a one-year contract with an option for one more year. He is the third-choice goalkeeper and had two league appearances for Milan as of 20 September 2025.

    Background

    Maignan was born in Cayenne in French Guiana and grew up in the Paris region. As a child he played for Villiers-le-Bel JS from 2003 to 2009, before his talent took him on to one of France's biggest academies.

    In 2009 he joined Paris Saint-Germain, where he played in the youth teams until 2012. He then moved up to the reserve team and stayed there until 2015 without breaking into the first team.

    Career

    Maignan left PSG in 2015 and joined Lille, where he first played for the reserves and then became first-choice goalkeeper. He was at the club until 2021 and won Ligue 1 in 2020/21, the season before the move to Milan.

    Milan agreed a five-year contract on 27 May 2021, effective from 1 July. The transfer fee is estimated at around €15 million, but some sources mention €13 million plus €2 million in bonuses. He quickly became first choice after Gianluigi Donnarumma and won Serie A in 2021/22 and the Supercoppa Italiana in 2024/25.

    He has 51 caps for France, and also 37 matches at youth level. He won the Nations League with France in 2021 and was named in the team of the tournament at Euro 2024. Individually he has been named Serie A's best goalkeeper 2021/22 and Ligue 1 Goalkeeper of the Year 2018/19.

    In 2024/25 he also played for Milan Futuro. From 7 July 2025 he was on loan at Lecce with an option to buy. Lecce triggered the option on 16 June 2026, and Milan used their buy-back clause two days later.

    He won the U17 European Championship in 2024 and was named the tournament's best player. For Italy he has two senior caps, and several matches from U15 to U21.
